FAQ

What is Narravue?

Narravue is an AI-visibility and reputation firm. It helps founders, executives and creators control how artificial intelligence describes them — diagnosing how AI engines read them today, then making them the name AI recommends.

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of shaping content and online presence so AI engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI cite and recommend you in their answers. Where SEO aimed to rank pages on a results list, GEO aims to make you the answer the AI gives.

Can you control what ChatGPT or AI says about me?

You cannot edit a model directly, but you can strongly influence what it says. AI engines build their answers from the sources they can find about you. By engineering accurate, consistent, authoritative content across the web, Narravue shapes the picture those engines form — making you visible, correctly represented and cited.

Who is Narravue for?

Narravue works with founders, executives, public-facing experts, and established creators and personal brands — people whose name is an asset and who need AI and the web to represent them accurately and favourably.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO optimises to rank a web page on a results list, where the user still chooses what to click. GEO optimises to be the source an AI cites inside a single generated answer, where there is no list and only a few names appear. Narravue focuses on the second, while keeping the search fundamentals that support it.

What does Narravue actually do?

Narravue runs one continuous engine: diagnose how AI and the web see you today, fix the gaps by engineering citation-worthy content and a consistent footprint, amplify your standing until you are the cited name, and defend it with ongoing monitoring.
